Permissions

Upload TIDP

Drawings already uploaded keep their status and tasks; their title and target date are updated. Drawings that are no longer in the sheet are kept but marked.

Remove TIDP list

Tasks already started or done are kept; only their link to the drawing is removed. This cannot be undone, but you can upload the sheet again.

Link the online sheet

Paste the share link of the Excel file on OneDrive (Share → Copy link, “Anyone with the link can view”) or a Google Sheet shared as “Anyone with the link”. The app reads it on its own every few minutes while someone who manages TIDP has the tracker open.

The sheet is treated as a pending list: drawings that leave it become Submitted, new rows are added, changed dates are updated, and a drawing that comes back becomes Revise. A “Status” column (Submitted / Approved / Revise / No need), if the sheet has one, is applied too.

Add a user

Engineer: takes tasks and updates only the tasks under the linked name. Team leader, Senior and Mid Senior: see, edit, add and delete the tasks of one discipline. Manager: sees and edits every task and the Management dashboard. Admin: everything, including users, team and projects. Only engineers take tasks. Give each person their email and password yourself; the app does not send emails.
